Design & digital products.

2025 – present

In 2025, I expanded into interface design after realizing solid backend architecture deserves an equally clear UI. Instead of reaching for bloated templates, I started designing my own layouts in Figma with a focus on typography, high contrast, and zero fluff.

Alongside freelance client work, I build and maintain my own tools — including BlockX 2.0 (an open-source content-filtering Chrome extension), a client-side 60 FPS WebCodecs video rendering engine in React, and short-form technical breakdowns on Linux security and open-source AI.

My beginnings.

2023 – 2024

I started coding in 2023 out of pure curiosity for how software works under the hood. Working on a Lenovo ThinkPad running Arch Linux, I spent my time learning core programming fundamentals, Python frameworks (Django, Flask, FastAPI), and system hardening.

What began as small scripts to automate repetitive tasks quickly grew into full-stack web applications like LingoQuest and GatherHub, teaching me how to take an idea from a blank terminal to production.
